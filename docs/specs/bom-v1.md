## Changes in v1.7

Adds the should parts confirmed on 2026-10-23 (BRD-057 NFC, BRD-058 haptics, BRD-059 key holder), decided by Claude under the maintainers' delegation from a parts bench (primary sources, stock captured 2026-10-23). Prototypes populate every option (BRD-053).

1. **Key holder (line 122): Infineon SLB9673**, TPM 2.0 on I2C2 at 0x2E, the only I2C TPM named in mainline `tpm_tis_i2c`; ST ST33KTPM2 as second source. Secure elements (NXP SE050, Microchip ATECC608B) were not chosen: no kernel interface, vendor middleware only, whereas a TPM works with the standard Debian tooling. It backs the keystore of OS-032 (blackbird-os ADR 0013).
2. **Haptics (lines 123–124): TI DRV2605L** (mainline since v4.0 or earlier, closed loop, auto-resonance) with a **Vybronics VG1040003D** coin LRA; DRV2624/2625 have no mainline driver, CS40L50 has no stock and needs firmware outside linux-firmware.
3. **NFC (lines 125–128):** a **dynamic tag, NXP NTAG I2C plus**, for tap-to-pair: no driver, under $1, readable with the T1 off, nothing transmitting near the audio chain. The **NXP PN7160** reader is the population option for T1-to-T1 hand-off (fitted on prototypes). Mainline Linux has no NFC card emulation, so the tag is the phone path in every variant.
4. **Kernel:** Debian arm64 builds neither `TCG_TIS_I2C` nor `INPUT_DRV260X_HAPTICS`: both ship as DKMS modules or in OpenDAP's kernel configuration (OS-023) until Debian enables them; `NFC_NXP_NCI_I2C` is already `=m`.
5. **I2C2 M0 addresses** added: 0x28 (PN7160), 0x2E (TPM), 0x55 (tag), 0x5A (haptic driver); all free on that bus.
6. **Cost (qty 1, est., USD):** about +6.5 in every variant (TPM 4.55, DRV2605L 1.23, tag 0.70, plus the LRA, price not published, and the antenna); +5.5 more for the PN7160 where fitted.

## Changes in v1.2

Power margin rule used here (BRD-049): continuous load ≤ 80 % of the rating for converters, regulators, switches and fuses (fuses ≤ 75 %), ≤ 70 % for connector contacts with all contacts loaded; the worst-case trip of the protecting current limiter ≤ 100 % of the rating. The full check is in [power tree §4.10](power-tree-v0.md#410-power-margin-check-bom-v12). Stock captured 2026-10-22 (LCSC through the JLCPCB parts API, Western distributors through FindChips).

1. **vcc3v3_sys buck (line 6): TLV62569DBVR (2 A) → TI TPS62826DMQR (3 A)**, 2.4–5.5 V in, 100 % duty cycle, power-good output (SLVSEF9I). Peak 1.85 A (est.) is 62 % of 3 A and the inductor peak 2.25 A is 61 % of its 3.7 A minimum switch limit; v1.1 ran at 80–90 % of the TLV62569. LCSC C2073319: 17,032; Mouser 7,156. The pin- and BOM-compatible TPS62827DMQR (4 A) is the drop-in if the schematic re-sum exceeds 2.4 A. Its 0.47 µH inductor is the DFE252012F-R47M already on line 4 (TI's recommended L–C matrix); the TPS62A03 fallback is dropped (thin stock).
2. **HAT power connector (line 66): DF40C-30 → DF40C-50 pair** (46 usable contacts). At 0.3 A per contact the 30-pin could not carry `HAT_5V` and `HAT_3V3` with their returns at 70 % (HAT_3V3 sat at 96 % of four contacts at its eFuse ceiling). The allocation (HAT_5V ×12, HAT_3V3 ×6, GND ×22, HAT_DET# ×1, reserved ×5) now comes from the hat-standard [HAT interface v0](https://github.com/opendap-project/hat-standard/blob/main/docs/specs/interface-v0.md) (Draft). ## 4. Protection, level shifting and clocks

### 4.1 ESD capacitance budget for high-speed lines

A shunt capacitance C on a line of characteristic impedance Z0 sees the two line halves in parallel, so its time constant is τ = (Z0/2)·C, and it adds a 10–90 % edge of about 2.2·τ.

| Interface | Z0 single-ended | Unit interval | C per line | 2.2·τ | Share of UI | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| USB 3 Gen 1, PCIe Gen 2 (5 Gb/s) | 45 Ω (90 Ω diff) | 200 ps | 0.5 pF (TPD4E05U06 typ) | 25 ps | 12 % | OK |
| same | 45 Ω | 200 ps | 1.0 pF | 50 ps | 25 % | Marginal; avoid |
| CSI-2 D-PHY (2.5 Gb/s per lane max) | 50 Ω | 400 ps | 0.8 pF (TPD4E1U06) | 44 ps | 11 % | OK; TPD4E05U06 used anyway for one part number |
| USB 2.0 HS (480 Mb/s) | 45 Ω | 2.08 ns | 0.5 pF | 25 ps | 1 % | OK |
| MIPI DSI to the faceplate (1 lane, ≤ 500 Mb/s) | 50 Ω | ≥ 2 ns | 0.5 pF | 28 ps | 1.4 % | OK |
| MIPI DSI, other faceplates (4 lanes, ≤ 1 Gb/s per lane, **UNVERIFIED** RK3566 maximum) | 50 Ω | 1 ns | 0.5 pF | 28 ps | 2.8 % | OK |
| Headphone outputs (audio) | — | — | 12 pF (TPD1E10B06) | — | — | 12 pF against the ≈ 0.5 Ω output impedance of a paralleled pair (1.0 Ω ballast per half, v1.3) is a 27 GHz pole: irrelevant |

The add-on connectors (flat cable, pogo contact) and the passive 2:1 muxes of the topology report add their own loss and capacitance; the ESD part is the smallest term at 0.5 pF.
