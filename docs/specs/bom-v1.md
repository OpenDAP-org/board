# BOM v1

- **Status:** BOM v1.2, cross-validated draft. v1.2 applies the maintainers' decisions of 2026-10-22 (power safety first, BRD-049; battery path for the full output budget, BRD-050; RGB status LED, FPT-025; HAT interface by function, HCS-021, HCS-022): [Changes in v1.2](#changes-in-v12). v1.1 closes the datasheet-review items of §9 that a part, value or circuit change can close ([Changes in v1.1](#changes-in-v11)); the rest stay *Open* with their blocker. v1 supersedes the [BOM v1 draft](bom-v1-draft.md) after the [cross-validation](../research/2026-10-bom-v1-cross-validation.md). Only lines marked *Decided* are decisions (ADRs). Every *Proposed* line still needs the maintainers' confirmation; *Open* lines have no compliant pick yet; **Blocker** marks a line that cannot meet the product rules today.
- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Scope:** the T1 main board and the touch faceplate, by subsystem, including the support parts around the main ICs (regulators, switches, protection, clocks, magnetics, connectors, test access).
- **Companion:** [power tree v0](power-tree-v0.md) (updated with this BOM) holds the rails, currents, gating map, sequencing and the calculations behind the power, audio, backlight and HAT picks.
- **Sources:** [BOM v1 draft](bom-v1-draft.md), [cross-validation](../research/2026-10-bom-v1-cross-validation.md), [BOM v0](bom-v0.md), [pin multiplexing v0](../research/2026-10-pin-multiplexing-v0.md), [component selection](../research/2026-10-component-selection.md), [sourcing alternatives](../research/2026-10-sourcing-alternatives.md), [high-speed I/O topology](../research/2026-10-high-speed-io-topology.md), [battery selection](../research/2026-10-battery-selection.md), touch-faceplate ADR 0001, board ADRs 0001–0009, opendap-os ADRs 0006–0008.
- **Product rules applied:** availability first and validated; newest compatible generation; mainline Linux drivers (DKMS or validated vendor drivers acceptable); no unusual switches or tricks (BRD-042); helper MCUs never in a data path (BRD-005); audio quality and battery life first; every block power-gated when unused (BRD-008); every bus and interface protected (BRD-007); the SoC reaches every chip directly (BRD-004).

## Conventions

- **Driver path:** *mainline + Debian* (module enabled in Debian's trixie-backports `linux-config-7.2_7.2.9-1~bpo13+1` arm64 config, re-checked 2026-10-22), *DKMS* (mainline driver Debian does not enable; opendap-os ADR 0007), *none* (no driver needed).
- **Availability:** stock and qty-1 price captured **2026-10-22**. LCSC figures come from the JLCPCB parts API; Mouser, Newark, Arrow, Verical and TME figures from [FindChips](https://www.findchips.com/) (brokers excluded); Digi-Key figures from Digi-Key pages. - **UNVERIFIED** = no primary source found in this session.
- Datasheets (URL, revision, date, sha256) are in the [Datasheets](#datasheets) table at the end. No PDF is stored in the repository.

## Changes in v1.2

Power margin rule used here (BRD-049): continuous load ≤ 80 % of the rating for converters, regulators, switches and fuses (fuses ≤ 75 %), ≤ 70 % for connector contacts with all contacts loaded; the worst-case trip of the protecting current limiter ≤ 100 % of the rating. The full check is in [power tree §4.10](power-tree-v0.md#410-power-margin-check-bom-v12). Stock captured 2026-10-22 (LCSC through the JLCPCB parts API, Western distributors through FindChips).

1. **vcc3v3_sys buck (line 6): TLV62569DBVR (2 A) → TI TPS62826DMQR (3 A)**, 2.4–5.5 V in, 100 % duty cycle, power-good output (SLVSEF9I). Peak 1.85 A (est.) is 62 % of 3 A and the inductor peak 2.25 A is 61 % of its 3.7 A minimum switch limit; v1.1 ran at 80–90 % of the TLV62569. LCSC C2073319: 17,032; Mouser 7,156. The pin- and BOM-compatible TPS62827DMQR (4 A) is the drop-in if the schematic re-sum exceeds 2.4 A. Its 0.47 µH inductor is the DFE252012F-R47M already on line 4 (TI's recommended L–C matrix); the TPS62A03 fallback is dropped (thin stock).
2. **HAT power connector (line 66): DF40C-30 → DF40C-50 pair** (46 usable contacts). At 0.3 A per contact the 30-pin could not carry `HAT_5V` and `HAT_3V3` with their returns at 70 % (HAT_3V3 sat at 96 % of four contacts at its eFuse ceiling). The allocation (HAT_5V ×12, HAT_3V3 ×6, GND ×22, HAT_DET# ×1, reserved ×5) now comes from the hat-standard [HAT interface v0](https://github.com/opendap-project/hat-standard/blob/main/docs/specs/interface-v0.md) (Draft). DF40C-50DP: Mouser 32,399; DF40C-50DS: Mouser 14,705, Future 45,000.
3. **HAT signal connector (line 66): 2 reserved contacts** (HCS-022) by dropping `CAM_PWDN` (expander line freed, line 93) and the optional HAT output on the DAC clock (one AXC bit freed, line 95). Contact positions are owned by the hat-standard spec; the board maps the RK3566 onto it ([pin plan §4.3](../research/2026-10-pin-multiplexing-v0.md#43-mapping-to-the-hat-interface-v0)).
4. **Battery (lines 46–47) and new board fuse (line 104):** [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-battery-path-rated-for-the-full-output-budget.md) (Proposed) defines the full output budget on battery (4.8 A from the cell at VSYS 3.3 V) and the pack acceptance criteria (≥ 6 A continuous, PCM trip ≥ 6 A, lead and connector ≥ 6.9 A per pole). **No stocked pack meeting them was found**, so the Adafruit 328 stays as the interim pack with the 1.6 A budget, now backed by a 2.5 A fuse; BRD-050 is not met yet (§7 item 3).
5. **Battery-path parts sized for the full budget:** BQ25895 6 A continuous discharge checked (80 %); input current limit capped at 15 W and 2.0 A (line 39); TPS2121 current limit set to 2.0–3.0 A (line 45); VSYS shunt ≥ 1 W (line 54); gauge sense resistor sized per pack (line 43).
6. **TPS61089 current limit (lines 91–92):** the datasheet allows the limit to sit 0.8 A below the calculated value (SLVSD38C §9.2.2.3), so 200 kΩ gives 4.35 A minimum, not 4.6 A; the 3.7 A peak was 85 % of it. RILIM 200 kΩ → **182 kΩ** (5.66 A typ, 4.86 A min): peak at 76 %.
7. **Status LED (lines 79, 105): RGB** (FPT-025). Everlight 19-337/R6GHBHC-A01/2T (red AlGaInP, green and blue InGaN, VF ≤ 3.8 V at 20 mA) on the faceplate, driven from the board by a **TI LP5521** I2C RGB driver whose 1.5× charge pump keeps the LED supply at ≥ 4.29 V down to VSYS = 3.0 V (0.29 V margin over the maximum VF and driver saturation at 20 mA). Mainline `leds-lp5521` (DKMS on Debian). The faceplate connector carries LED_R, LED_G, LED_B (2 spares used, 5 left, line 71). The yellow-green v1.1 part is withdrawn.
8. **HP-amp +5 V LDO (line 25): margin finding.** The worst continuous sine (balanced, 32 Ω, both channels, 122 mA) is 81 % of the TPS7A4901's 150 mA. Proposed fix: the LT3045 (500 mA) with its negative twin LT3094 (500 mA) for matched soft-start, both stocked; **pending** their datasheet review (ADI PDFs not retrieved this session) and the maintainers' confirmation, because it touches the audio stage (§7 item 13).
9. **MaskROM gate input (lines 78, 98):** the SN74LVC1G57 low threshold is guaranteed only below 0.35 V at 1.65 V; behind the BAT54 (0.24–0.32 V) the margin is 30–110 mV. The PWRON_N pull-up is set to **20 kΩ** to vcca1v8_pmu (≈ 0.09 mA), which keeps the diode current near 0.1 mA, where the 0.24–0.32 V drop applies.
10. **Debian config re-check:** `GPIO_PCA953X=y` (line 93, was **UNVERIFIED**); `LEDS_LP55XX_COMMON`, `LEDS_LP5521` not set (DKMS).

## 0. Changes from the draft

Full evidence and severity in the [cross-validation report](../research/2026-10-bom-v1-cross-validation.md) §2.

1. **MCLK oscillators (lines 20, 21): decided in [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-audio-master-clock-source.md)** (stocked 3.3 V NDK NSC5083D on a dedicated LDO, passive attenuator into the 1.8 V XTI input). The 1.8 V NSC5083A has no authorized stock, and no stocked audio-grade 1.8 V oscillator exists at 22.5792 MHz.
2. **Battery (line 46) follows ADR 0007:** Adafruit 328 primary, Adafruit 2011 second source. The quoted 105060 pack is removed (T1-038). The 2-pin JST PH connector (line 47) is the single footprint; the board NTC stays.
3. **Battery current budget:** the pack (2C max) and its JST PH lead (2 A per contact) cap the battery path at 2.0 A continuous. VSYS is no longer rated 3.5 A on battery; HAT and USB-C source power on battery are software-budgeted (power tree §4.6).
4. **New HAT/USB 5 V boost (new line 91, TPS61088):** the BQ25895 cannot run OTG while it is charging, so the draft could not power a class A/B HAT while USB-C is plugged in. HAT 5 V and USB-C source power now come from a VSYS boost behind the eFuses.
5. **TPS2121 input isolation (line 45):** both OV inputs are driven during any 5 V sourcing, otherwise the T1's own 5 V re-enters the charger through IN1 or IN2.
6. **vdd_cpu buck (line 3): RK860-2 → RK860-0**, the variant on the RK3566 reference (Radxa ZERO 3W), with better stock (LCSC 4,528).
7. **IO domains corrected to the pin plan:** PMUIO2 (I2C0/1/2) at 1.8 V, faceplate bank VCCIO5 at 3.3 V. The draft §4.2 had I2C0 at 3.3 V and the faceplate at 1.8 V, which contradicts the pin plan and the DAC's 1.8 V-only I2C on the same PMUIO2 bank.
8. **Faceplate connector: FH35C-45S → FH35C-51S** (44 used + 7 spare = 14 %, ADR 0006), stocked at Mouser.
9. **HAT flat cable (line 66) picked:** Hirose DF40 0.4 mm board-to-FPC pair, 100-pin signal + 30-pin power, stocked in thousands.
10. **Missing parts added:** GPIO expander TCA6416A, HAT ID translator PCA9306, HAT audio translators (SN74AXC8T245, SN74AXC4T774, SN74AXC2T45), MaskROM combo circuit (TPS3839 + SN74LVC1G27 + BAT54 + DMG1012T), faceplate load switch FP_3V3, panel RST/TE translators on the faceplate, 4.4 mm fallback jack as primary.
11. **ESD counts corrected:** faceplate DSI + low-speed and HAT low-speed lines were under-counted.
12. **4.4 mm jack (line 31):** the stocked G-Switch part becomes primary (T1-029); genuine Pentaconn stays a quoted upgrade.
13. **Second sources removed when incompatible:** INA226 (I2C VIH too high for a 1.8 V bus), Alps/Panasonic side switches against a top-actuated primary.
14. **Item tracking (BRD-030) has no hardware line yet:** new *Open* line 103.

## 1. What changed from v0

1. **Master clocks: 45.1584/49.152 MHz → 22.5792/24.576 MHz, 1.8 V.** The CS43198 accepts an external MCLK only at 22.5792 or 24.576 MHz (DS1156F2 Table 4-3), and its XTI/MCLK input is in the 1.8 V VL domain (VL 1.66–1.94 V). BRD-023 is corrected (Confirmed). See power tree §1.
2. **Battery line replaced** by the off-the-shelf Adafruit 328 (ADR 0007). The v0 Jauch LP705176JS fits no envelope.
3. **HAT pogo pins move to the device-side piece** (board ADR 0002, flat cable). The board now carries the flat-cable connector instead; the pogo line is kept for traceability.
4. **eFuses ×2 → ×3:** a third TPS259470 limits and protects the USB-C VBUS source path (OTG host).
5. **New support parts** around every main IC: vdd_cpu buck, LPDDR4X 0.6 V LDO, external 3.3 V buck, audio rails (TPS65131, TPS7A4901, TPS7A3001, TPS62840, TPS7A2018), 5 V boost, load switches, backlight driver, current monitors, ESD/TVS, translators, GPIO expander, crystals, inductors, connectors, buttons and test access.

## 4. Protection, level shifting and clocks

### 4.1 ESD capacitance budget for high-speed lines

A shunt capacitance C on a line of characteristic impedance Z0 sees the two line halves in parallel, so its time constant is τ = (Z0/2)·C, and it adds a 10–90 % edge of about 2.2·τ.

| Interface | Z0 single-ended | Unit interval | C per line | 2.2·τ | Share of UI | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| USB 3 Gen 1, PCIe Gen 2 (5 Gb/s) | 45 Ω (90 Ω diff) | 200 ps | 0.5 pF (TPD4E05U06 typ) | 25 ps | 12 % | OK |
| same | 45 Ω | 200 ps | 1.0 pF | 50 ps | 25 % | Marginal; avoid |
| CSI-2 D-PHY (2.5 Gb/s per lane max) | 50 Ω | 400 ps | 0.8 pF (TPD4E1U06) | 44 ps | 11 % | OK; TPD4E05U06 used anyway for one part number |
| USB 2.0 HS (480 Mb/s) | 45 Ω | 2.08 ns | 0.5 pF | 25 ps | 1 % | OK |
| MIPI DSI to the faceplate (1 lane, ≤ 500 Mb/s) | 50 Ω | ≥ 2 ns | 0.5 pF | 28 ps | 1.4 % | OK |
| MIPI DSI, other faceplates (4 lanes, ≤ 1 Gb/s per lane, **UNVERIFIED** RK3566 maximum) | 50 Ω | 1 ns | 0.5 pF | 28 ps | 2.8 % | OK |
| Headphone outputs (audio) | — | — | 12 pF (TPD1E10B06) | — | — | 12 pF against a 1 Ω output impedance is a 13 GHz pole: irrelevant |

The add-on connectors (flat cable, pogo contact) and the passive 2:1 muxes of the topology report add their own loss and capacitance; the ESD part is the smallest term at 0.5 pF.

## 7. Open questions

Closed by the cross-validation: BRD-023 frequencies (confirmed), MaskROM entry (ADR 0009, lines 97–98), battery connector (2-pin JST PH, ADR 0007), HAT flat-cable connector (line 66), HAT ESD location (pogo field), backlight driver (board).

1. **MCLK (lines 20–21):** the passive attenuator into the 1.8 V XTI input must be confirmed by SPICE before layout freeze (ADR 0010).
2. **3.5 mm output topology:** parallel halves for 16 Ω drive (4 packages) or one half per channel?
3. **HAT power on battery (BRD-050, v1.2):** [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-battery-path-rated-for-the-full-output-budget.md) (Proposed) sizes the board for the full budget (4.8 A from the battery) and sets the pack criteria, but no stocked pack meets them, so the interim Adafruit 328 keeps ≈ 0.4 A at 5.1 V on battery. Maintainers: accept ADR 0011 and its definition of the full budget (1.5 A at 5.1 V shared by HAT_5V and USB-C, plus HAT_3V3 1.0 A; both 5 V ports at 1.5 A together would need ≈ 7.3 A, above the BQ25895's 6 A), and choose the fallback if no pack is found (lower on-battery budget, a quoted pack against T1-038, or another charger and cell format).
4. **Item tracking with the T1 off (BRD-030, line 103):** required, or only while the T1 is on or suspended?
5. **Action button (line 76):** sample deeper-travel switches with the case plunger.
6. **DF40 at 5 Gb/s (line 66):** confirm the Hirose rating and build a test coupon before freeze.
7. **Shielding cans:** fit by default, or only after EMC and audio measurements?
8. **RTC across battery swaps:** accept that the RK809 RTC loses time when the battery is removed (RV-3028-C7 has LCSC 0 stock, so no stocked backup RTC was found)?
9. **FPC as a fabricated part (line 102):** confirm that impedance-controlled FPCs count like the PCB under T1-038.
10. **MCLK supply at 3.1 V (line 29, v1.1):** ADR 0010 amended to the TPS7A2031; the phase-noise check at 3.1 V stays for the first boards.
11. **Status LED (lines 79, 105, v1.2):** RGB through the LP5521 replaces the yellow-green part. It uses 2 of the 7 faceplate spares (5 left, 9.8 %): accept, or move to the 61-contact FH35C if the ≈ 10 % spare rule must hold strictly (FH35C-61S had 0 authorized stock, line 66).
